# Social Media Do's and Don'ts

## ✅ Do's
- Use strong privacy settings to control who can see your posts
- Share positive, respectful, and professional content
- Think before posting and consider long-term impact
- Verify information before sharing to avoid spreading misinformation
- Use strong and unique passwords for your accounts

## ❌ Don'ts
- Do not share personal information like phone number or address publicly
- Avoid posting offensive, harmful, or inappropriate content
- Do not click on suspicious links or unknown messages
- Avoid engaging in cyberbullying or online arguments
- Do not share your live location or travel plans publicly
