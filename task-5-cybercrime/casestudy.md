# Phishing Cybercrime Case Study

Phishing is a type of cybercrime in which attackers try to trick people into sharing sensitive information such as passwords, bank details, or OTPs by pretending to be a trusted organization.

A common real-life scenario involves users receiving messages that appear to be from banks or payment services. For example, in India, several cases have been reported where individuals received SMS or WhatsApp messages claiming that their bank account or UPI service would be blocked. The message included a link asking users to “verify” their details urgently.

When users clicked on the link, they were redirected to a fake website that looked identical to the official banking site. After entering their login details or OTP, the attackers gained access to their accounts and performed unauthorized transactions.

Students and young users are often targeted because they may not verify links carefully or may panic due to urgent messages. In many reported cases, victims lost money within minutes of sharing their information.

The consequences of phishing include financial loss, identity theft, and misuse of personal data. It can also lead to long-term risks if the same passwords are used across multiple platforms.

This case highlights the importance of being cautious while clicking links and never sharing confidential information online.
