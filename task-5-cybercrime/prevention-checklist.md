# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

- Do not click on suspicious links or unknown emails
- Always verify website URLs before entering details
- Never share OTP, passwords, or bank details with anyone
- Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts
- Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions
- Regularly update apps and software to fix security vulnerabilities
- Check for HTTPS (secure connection) before entering sensitive information”
- Be cautious of messages creating urgency or fear
- For UPI safety: Always verify payment requests before approving
- For UPI safety: Never scan unknown QR codes
